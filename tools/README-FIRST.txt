Sightline ATV Safety Academy - running it on your own computer

1. Unzip this folder somewhere simple, like your Desktop or C:\Sightline.
2. Open the folder and start it:
     Windows:  double-click  START_SIGHTLINE.bat
     Mac:      right-click  START_SIGHTLINE.command  and choose Open
               (the first time only; after that a double-click works)
3. A black window opens and sets things up. The first time takes 5 to 15
   minutes and needs the internet. When it is ready your browser opens the
   site at http://localhost:8080 and the window shows your sign-in email and
   a one-time password.
4. To stop the site, double-click STOP_SIGHTLINE. To start it again, use
   START_SIGHTLINE; later starts take a few seconds.

Your copy keeps its own accounts and progress in the "data" folder inside this
one. Nothing you do here changes the live website.

Ranger, the tutor, answers from the course text on its own. To put it on the
full AI model, double-click ADD_RANGER_KEY and paste the key you were given.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
"Run anyway". If a Mac says the file "cannot be opened", use right-click and
Open as in step 2.
