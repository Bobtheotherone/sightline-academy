# Round-3 review dossier — sort-cond-cold-hands.svg

Final verdict after 3 rounds: **fix**

## Outstanding defects (fix every P1 and P2; P3 where cheap)
### 1. [P1] gloved hand — the four finger lobes (svg y 40.5–49.9, x 24–50) against the brake-lever blade (y 41.4–45.8)
- Where: renders/sort-cond-cold-hands.zoom.png and the 'lever thru fingers' crop (svg x 20–64, y 36–54); same read in .true.png @40 px and @32 px DPR1
- What: Nothing is being gripped. The four fingers are straight, parallel, equal-width (5 u) round-tipped tines drawn IN FRONT of the lever, hanging 3–4 u past its lower edge with no wrap, no crook and no contact. Compounding it, the 2-unit navy slots between the fingers are the same colour and near the same weight as the 4.3-unit solid navy lever, so blade and finger-gaps fuse into one navy grid. The result reads as a hand resting on a rail, or as a comb/rake — i.e. the same straight-parallel-lobe 'paw' construction as the retired icon and as sort-gear-gloves, which is precisely the defect §H was opened to fix. §H acceptance line 1 ('a hand on a lever') is not delivered.
- Fix: Curl the fingers: end each tip on an arc that turns back inboard (+x) and stops at or above y=45 so the tips sit on the lever, not below it; then redraw the lever's outboard third (x≈5–24) IN FRONT of the fingertips so the blade visibly passes through a closed fist. Vary the finger widths (index widest, pinky narrowest) and break the dead-straight sides with a first-knuckle bend. Drop the blade to ~3 u thick so it is no longer the same visual weight as the slots between the fingers.

### 2. [P1] handlebar grip (#2F6B52 capsule x 11–33, y 22–35, three 1.5-u ribs) versus the lever rod (x 3.4–60)
- Where: renders/sort-cond-cold-hands.true.png @40 px; 32 px DPR1 contact sheet; 'thumb+grip junction' crop (svg x 8–34, y 18–42)
- What: The hierarchy is inverted, so both objects read as the wrong thing. The lever is the longest, heaviest, full-width horizontal in the frame (with a ball tip left and a bolted boss right), while the handlebar is a short dark capsule that stops dead inside the hand. A learner therefore reads the navy rod as the handlebar and has nothing left to read as a lever; the spruce capsule with its four vertical bars clustered in the outboard third reads as a separate object — at 32 px the ribs fuse into a dark core and it reads as a battery / flashlight / grille. At true size the icon says 'a mitten over a bar', not 'a hand on a brake lever'.
- Fix: Make the bar the dominant horizontal: one spruce tube running from the bar-end cap at x≈6, behind the fist, and out the right side to x≈58, with the sage hand drawn over it. Delete the rib cluster or reduce it to two ribs at 1.8 u spanning only the visible outboard section (1.5 u is under the set's interior minimum and aliases to a smudge at 40 px). Then hang the lever off that tube on a small navy perch block at x≈50 and taper the blade down-and-outboard from it, ending inboard of the fist's outer edge.

### 3. [P2] the two #1E8A6E snow asterisks (x 4.9–12.1 y 6.3–14.7, and x 18.4–23.6 y 5–11, stroke 1.8)
- Where: 40 px DPR1 render (snow crop, x24) and the 32 px DPR1 contact sheet; the harness's DPR-2 .true.png flatters them
- What: The cold marks do not survive true size. The second flake is 6 u across on 1.8-u strokes (arm-to-weight 3.3:1), so at 40 px DPR1 it fills in completely and renders as a solid teal square blob. At 32 px — the size the sort component uses for a placed chip (size-8 in sort_categorize/index.tsx:169) — both flakes lose their diagonals and read as two teal crosses, which in a safety course reads as first-aid crosses, not snow. §H's acceptance ('a hand on a lever WITH COLD MARKS') fails at both shipped sizes, and U10 (legible at smallest intended size) fails.
- Fix: One flake, not two, ≥9 u across at 2.2 u weight — sort-cond-frost's small flake is 10 u at 2.2 and survives 32 px. Put it in the empty top-right quadrant. Re-check at 32 px DPR1, not only the harness's DPR-2 .true.png.

### 4. [P2] lever pivot boss circle (57,43.6) r3 with #F9FCFA bolt r1.3, and the right-hand termination of the handlebar
- Where: 'right of hand: bar absent' crop (svg x 44–64, y 14–50); visible in .zoom.png
- What: The pivot sits on blank paper — no perch, no clamp, nothing above or behind it — while the handlebar has two ends (a rounded cap at x=11 and an end swallowed by the hand at x=33) and never reaches the machine. - Fix: Carry the bar through to x≈58 as in the P1 fix and clamp the pivot to it with a ~6×8 navy perch block so the blade has something to hinge on. Delete the white bolt dot: r1.3 is 0.8 px at 40 px and does not survive anyway.

### 5. [P2] thumb lobe path M27 27.4 C23 25.6 19.6 27.2 19 31 C18.4 34.8 21.6 38 26.2 37.2 Z

- Fix: Move the thumb below the bar so it wraps from the far side, tip pointing down-right at about (26,40); open its top edge into the hand's outline instead of closing the lobe; taper it from root to tip; and confirm no paper is trapped between thumb, grip and palm.

## Reviewer notes
Round 3 review, nothing changed on disk. Ran _check.py (OK: 2218 bytes, viewBox 64x64, icon, animated=False) and _render.py --icon 40, then viewed all four renders: .img.png, .rm.png, .true.png, .zoom.png. Because the harness passes --icon as the width, .img/.rm/.true are the same 40 px shot (identical md5), so the only real inspection surfaces are .true.png and .zoom.png; I generated four extra views to compensate — 512 px for construction, 40 px and 32 px at DPR 1 (the harness renders at DPR 2, which hides the small-size failures), three 8 px/unit crops of the thumb-grip junction, the lever-through-fingers band and the area right of the hand, and 40/32 px contact sheets against the whole m5-l1-s2 deck plus sort-gear-gloves.

Two things the brief did not anticipate. First, 40 px is not the smallest shipped size: web/src/activities/sort_categorize/index.tsx renders the icon at size-10 (40 px) in the tray and size-8 (32 px) on the placed chip, so U10 must be judged at 32 px — where this icon is the weakest of the eight cards in its deck (frost, dust, wet-roots, fading-light and swollen-creek all still read; this one goes to 'a pale mitten, a dark green thing, two teal crosses'). Second, a set-level fact worth an owner decision: the shipped 26-icon sort set uses only #0D1E2E, #ABCDB8, #A93226, #DBA12E — the brief's mandated #2F6B52 and #1E8A6E are new to the set, and sort-cond-frost draws snow navy in the same deck. That conflict should be settled before this or §I/§J land, since §J will bring teal rain in too.

One claim I tested and dropped: the lever blade tip is flush with the bar end's outer edge (3.4 vs 3.5), not past it, so it is not a defect.

Verdict is fix on two P1s. The colour defect §H was opened for is genuinely gone, but the geometry defect underneath it is not: this is still a sage lobed mitten in the sort-gear-gloves / retired-icon construction family, now laid over two unconnected horizontal bars, and at 32–40 px neither 'lever' nor 'handlebar' arrives. The single highest-leverage change is to run one spruce bar all the way through the fist and out both sides, hang a tapered blade off a perch on it, and curl the fingertips onto that blade — after which the flake, thumb and crease fixes are small.
