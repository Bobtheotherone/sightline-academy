### 1. [P1] Whole emblem vs the level ladder (SET-B-LEVEL Acceptance: "shown in sequence on one strip, the seven read as ascending"; "Increasing structural complexity"; §L acceptance "reads as more advanced than level 5 / less than level 7")
- Where: 7-emblem contact strip at 96 px and 56 px (scratchpad/sheet.png) and the dashboard mock (scratchpad/ctx.png), levels 5-6-7 in order
- What: The strip does not read as ascending through 6. Levels 4, 5 and 7 are composed scenes that fill the disc; the candidate's drawn subject is the r40 card, 65.6 % of the sibling diameter, and the r40→r61 paper annulus (57 % of the disc's area) carries nothing but 2–3.5 %-contrast hairlines. Levels 1–3 also use paper, but their content crosses it, so this is the only emblem with a dead concentric band. Between Ridge Runner and Trail Boss it reads as a category break and a density dip, not a rank above 5. VISUAL_ASSETS Part 5 also requires the entry's own Acceptance to pass, and this is that entry's Acceptance.
- Fix: Fill the annulus with the set's landform language so 6 out-densities 5 while staying under 7: two nested closed contour terraces (#ECF3EF then #ABCDB8, navy 1.5 edges, apex low so the r40 card does not eat them — the retired file's blob contours survived the card where peaks did not), clipped to the r61 disc, with the compass card sitting over them. Keep the rose and the single magenta north arm unchanged.

### 2. [P2] Compass card — the solid #2F6B52 disc r40 inside the navy r61 rim (concentric ring pair)
- Where: renders/level-6-wayfinder.true.png at 56 px equivalent (wf56.true.png) and the dashboard adjacency mock, scratchpad/ctx.png, row "Wayfinder"
- What: On DashboardPage the emblem renders at size-14 (56 px) 24 px to the right of ProgressRing (Progress.tsx: 64 px, 5-unit #2F6B52 arc on a #D6DFDA track). Side by side, a solid pine-700 disc inside a rim reads as the same meter filled to 100 % — the eye groups them as "ring, ring". If the card must stay bare, break the concentricity read by letting a landform edge cross the card's rim (a contour band passing behind and out the other side).

### 3. [P2] Graduation system — sage r34 ring plus the eight sage ticks, all stroke-width 1.5
- Where: 56 px render (wf56.true.png, DPR2 = 0.875 device px per unit); radial pixel probes at N/E/S/W and NE
- What: At the dashboard size every sage mark falls under one device pixel and washes out unevenly: the four cardinal ticks measure #80AB95 (≈65 % of sage over the #2F6B52 card) while the four diagonals measure #568A72 (≈31 %), and the r34 ring itself drops in and out around the circle. Eight graduations therefore read as four marks plus four smudges over a pale haze, and "graduated card" — the thing that says compass rather than star — does not survive to 56 px. At 96 px the disparity is milder but still present (cardinals 100 % sage, diagonals ≈91 %, so the diagonals look thinner).
- Fix: Raise the ring and the ticks to 2.4–2.6 units. They are interior detail, not the file's outline, so this does not break the one-outline-weight rule (§0.2 sets the 1.5 outline for a 128 icon and forbids anything under 1.5, not anything over). If eight marks at that weight read heavy, cut to four cardinal ticks at 2.6 and keep the ring at 2.4.

### 4. [P3] Tick outer ends against the card rim (path d="M64 29.5 V25.8 …", r 34.5→38.2 with 0.75 round caps) vs the r40 rim
- Where: renders/level-6-wayfinder.zoom.png; radial probes at 96 px (N: #ABCDB8 at r 39.0, navy at r 39.5–40.5) and 56 px (E/W: rim #68747D at the tick, #132533 between ticks)
- What: The file's comment claims the ticks "stop 0.3 clear of the card rim". 0.3 units is 0.45 device px at 96 and 0.26 at 56 — no gap survives at either shipped size. Because the tick inner ends are also buried inside the r34 ring band by design, ring + ticks + rim fuse into one connected shape and the bezel reads as a spoked wheel rather than graduations sitting inside a bezel. At 56 px the tick antialiasing additionally lightens the navy rim at all eight positions, nicking the bezel.
- Fix: Float the ticks: run them r 35.5 → 37.6 (≈1.6 units, 1.4 device px clear of the rim inner edge at 56 px, and clear of the ring band on the inside). Combine with the weight increase above so the shorter marks still read.
